Tournament Structure

Full Information & 2027 Format

Stage 1 • Oct 2 – Oct 6

Super Series

The tournament kicks off in Windhoek, Namibia with the 3 lowest-ranked qualified teams playing a round-robin series. The winner advances to the main Group Stage.

Stage 2 • Oct 7 – Oct 24

Group Stage (12 Teams)

12 teams are divided into two groups of 6. Each team plays 5 group matches. The top 3 from each group plus the best 4th-placed team advance.

Stage 3 • Oct 25 – Nov 14

Super 7 Stage

The 7 surviving teams compete in a single round-robin league consisting of 21 high-stakes matches. The top 4 teams qualify for the Semi-Finals.

Stage 4 • Nov 17 – Nov 21

Knockouts & Final

Semi-Final 1 takes place in Cape Town (Nov 17), Semi-Final 2 in Centurion (Nov 18), and the Grand Final at The Wanderers, Johannesburg on Nov 21, 2027.
